## What this is

Marthak runs on **Shopify**. Maretti Sette runs on **WordPress and WooCommerce**. Both use **Courier Guy** already. Today, shipping means retyping orders into the courier app and copying tracking back into the store. This proposal automates that and adds a few alerts and emails so Martine spends less time on admin and more on packing and customer care.

---

## What Martine’s day looks like after setup

1. Check a short morning email (new orders, anything still unfulfilled, low stock if configured).  
2. Print shipping labels from Shopify or Woo (no Courier Guy app for normal orders).  
3. Pack and hand parcels to the daily collection.  
4. Handle returns and special cases on WhatsApp as today.

---

## What we will deliver

### Marthak (Shopify) first

**Shipping**

- Connect Courier Guy to Shopify.  
- Map **Home Delivery** and **Postnet Office** to the right courier options.  
- Add SKUs and weights where missing so labels and rates work reliably.  
- Create waybills from orders, add tracking, and send the usual customer shipping email.  
- Set up a simple **daily batch** (same rhythm as today: one collection, many orders).  
- Short written guide for a normal day and for Postnet orders.

**Alerts**

- Email when an order is paid (operations inbox).  
- Reminder if an order is still unfulfilled after 24 hours.  
- Low-stock warnings on products you choose.  
- Optional alert for high-value orders (amount agreed with you).

**Marketing (light touch)**

- Turn on abandoned checkout recovery in Shopify.  
- One post-purchase thank-you email (wording agreed with Martine).

### Maretti Sette (WooCommerce)

Same shipping and alert behaviour as Marthak, using the WooCommerce Courier Guy integration and tools already on the site.

### Handover

- Up to 90 minutes walkthrough with Martine or Amina.  
- 30 days after go-live: we fix issues with the configuration we delivered (not unrelated site changes).
