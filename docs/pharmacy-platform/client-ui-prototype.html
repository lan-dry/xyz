What your customers will see

A licensed-pharmacy-grade ordering channel: stock from your PMS, RSSB-ready checkout, and every prescription reviewed by a named pharmacist before dispense.

Order medicines online — with trust built in

Patients browse on their phone, choose your branch, pay or co-pay with mobile money, and track pickup or delivery. Your team uses a simple staff console tied to the same stock and receipts you already run in-store.

  • Real-time catalog synced from your pharmacy management system
  • Prescription upload with pharmacist approval (GDP/GPP aligned workflow)
  • RSSB and private insurance co-pay split at checkout
  • Pickup codes, delivery zones, and audit trail for disputes
  • English & French at launch; Kinyarwanda when you supply translations
  • Phone OTP login, saved addresses, dependent orders for family Rx
  • Delivery zones, cold-chain rules, proof of delivery
  • Cancellations, partial refunds, and complaint log
  • Owner dashboard: schemes, formulary upload, branch hours, audit export
