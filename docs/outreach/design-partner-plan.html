Salanor Design Partner Outreach Plan

Prepared for Landry Bougang Fotso · Salanor · May 2026 · Confidential working doc

1. Goal (90 days)

  • 10 intro calls with agent-platform teams
  • 4 serious pilot conversations
  • 2 signed letters of intent (LOI) for the design-partner program (3 = stretch)

Geography (Africa) is not a blocker if you lead with spec + SDK + a short demo video. Offer EU morning and US afternoon slots.

2. What you need before first outreach (minimum bar)

You do NOT need full production deploy on day one. You need a credible “try it” path:
  • Live marketing site with /spec and /contact
  • Public GitHub repo: aegis-sdk (TypeScript) + quickstart
  • Either: hosted pilot API or clear “run locally in 15 min” doc + Loom demo
  • One-page design partner offer (pricing, feedback cadence, non-binding LOI template)

3. One-line pitch

“APS-1 is an open standard for signed agent provenance — we help MCP and LangGraph teams prove what their agents did, for compliance and customer trust.”

6. Email template (short)

Subject: Signed provenance for [their product] agents (30 min?)

Hi [Name],

I’m building Salanor — open APS-1 events + Aegis so teams can cryptographically sign agent actions (tool calls, policy decisions) and verify them later.

Spec: salanor.com/spec · SDK: github.com/salanor/aegis-sdk

We’re taking 5 design partners for a 30-day pilot on one production trace. Would a 30-minute call next week work?

— Landry

7. Call agenda (30 min)

  1. Their agent stack and compliance pressure (5 min)
  2. 90s demo: sign → ingest → console trace (10 min)
  3. Integration effort for one agent (10 min)
  4. Design partner terms + next step (5 min)

8. Design partner LOI (plain English)

  • Non-binding intent to pilot Aegis on one agent/workflow for ~30 days
  • Partner provides feedback (biweekly 30 min)
  • Salanor provides discounted or free pilot tier + implementation support
  • Optional logo / quote after success

9. Metrics to track

  • Outreach sent / replies / calls booked / pilots started / LOIs signed
  • Time-to-first-signed-event for each pilot
