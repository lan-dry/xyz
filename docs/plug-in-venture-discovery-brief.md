## 1. Executive summary (30 seconds)

After pivoting from **Aether** (environmental monitoring) to **Aegis** (audit & traceability for AI agents in regulated finance)—with Kevin’s encouragement—I conducted **16 structured discovery conversations** across Rwanda and Europe.

**Main finding:** The problem is **real and understood**, but most local enterprises are **not yet at the automation stage** where an audit/control layer becomes urgent. They view Aegis as **technically interesting** and **strategically relevant**, but **not implementable today** because they lack the underlying agent/automation workflows Aegis is designed to govern.

This is **not a null result**. It is **market-timing and ICP clarity**: we learned *who* is ready, *who* is not, and *why*.

**Current focus:** Validate demand with **automation-active** prospects (EU regulated finance via design partner/reseller path) while continuing buyer interviews with entities further along the automation curve.

## 3. Discovery methodology

### What counted as an interview
- 20–45 minute conversations (in person, video, or phone)
- Structured around **problem validation**, not product demo-first
- Documented: role, company type, automation maturity, pain, objections, willingness to pay, referral to next contact

### Who was reached (16 total)

| Segment | Approx. count | Role |
|---------|---------------|------|
| **Managers / decision-makers** | ~9 | Compliance, operations, digital, general management |
| **Technical implementers** | ~7 | Developers, IT, engineers (often referred by managers) |
| **Europe (outbound)** | Several contacted | **No substantive responses to date** |

### Pattern observed
Several manager-level contacts **redirected to technical teams**—a signal that automation/AI governance is seen as an **IT topic**, not yet a **compliance/budget** topic in these organizations.

---

## 4. What we heard (synthesized findings)

### Finding A — Problem recognition (positive)
- Decision-makers and technical staff **understand** why audit trails matter for automated decisions
- Interest in AI and automation is **conceptual and positive**
- No one disputed that **accountability** will become mandatory as automation scales

### Finding B — Implementation gap (critical)
Repeated message across interviews:

> *“This makes sense, but we don’t have automation/agent workflows in production yet. Your tool controls something we haven’t built. We’d need automation first—then a control layer.”*

**Interpretation:** Aegis is perceived as **Layer 2** (governance/audit) while most local interviewees are still at **Layer 0–1** (manual processes, early digitalization, no agentic workflows).

### Finding C — Buying process clarity
- **Developers** find it interesting but **do not control budget** or compliance mandates
- **Managers** engage intellectually but **defer to technical teams** for feasibility
- **Neither group** has an active project budget for AI agent governance **today**

### Finding D — Geographic signal
| Market | Signal |
|--------|--------|
| **Rwanda (primary discovery)** | High interest, **low near-term readiness** — automation not yet deployed |
| **Europe (outbound)** | **No responses yet** — channel not validated; needs warm intros or partner-led access |

### Finding E — Partner pipeline (France)
- **France Lampe:** Integration discussions advanced; partner **later withdrew**
- **Jacob (JMT-S):** Re-engaged; proposes **reseller model** in France, citing regulatory pressure and domain knowledge
- **Gap:** Reseller enthusiasm ≠ validated end-customer WTP; still need **direct buyer conversations** with automation-active firms

## 5. Strategic conclusion — this **is** a discovery result

In lean startup terms, this is **negative validation on the current ICP and timing**—which is **valuable**:

| Question | Answer from 16 interviews |
|----------|---------------------------|
| Is AI accountability a real concern? | **Yes** (conceptually) |
| Are local enterprises ready to **buy** an audit layer now? | **Mostly no** — prerequisite automation missing |
| Is the buyer the developer? | **No** — but developers are often the only contact granted |
| Is Rwanda the right **first** beachhead for this SKU? | **Questionable near-term** — maturity curve lag |
| Is the idea dead? | **No** — **timing and segment** need adjustment |

**Revised thesis:**

> Aegis is not a “convince them AI is coming” product. It is a **“you already run agents—prove every action”** product.  
> **ICP shift:** Organizations with **automation already in production** (EU regulated finance, integrators’ clients, advanced fintech)—not enterprises still planning automation.
